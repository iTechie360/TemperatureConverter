# iTechie 360 Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

At **iTechie 360**, we are committed to fostering an open, welcoming, professional, and inclusive environment where everyone can contribute confidently regardless of experience, background, identity, or perspective.

We believe that innovation thrives when people collaborate with mutual respect, professionalism, and integrity.

By participating in this organization and its repositories, you agree to uphold this Code of Conduct.

---

## Our Values

Every member of the iTechie 360 community is expected to demonstrate the following values:

- **Integrity** – Be honest, transparent, and accountable.
- **Respect** – Treat everyone with dignity and courtesy.
- **Professionalism** – Communicate constructively and collaborate effectively.
- **Innovation** – Encourage creativity and continuous improvement.
- **Excellence** – Strive for high-quality work and engineering best practices.
- **Collaboration** – Share knowledge and help others succeed.
- **Responsibility** – Take ownership of your contributions and decisions.

---

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful in discussions.
- Providing constructive feedback.
- Welcoming new contributors.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully.
- Asking questions respectfully.
- Helping others learn and grow.
- Writing maintainable, secure, and well-documented code.
- Following project standards and contribution guidelines.
- Giving proper credit for the work of others.
- Protecting confidential information and intellectual property.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are unacceptable:

- Harassment or discrimination of any kind.
- Personal attacks, insults, or trolling.
- Hate speech.
- Bullying or intimidation.
- Deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private or confidential information without permission.
- Plagiarism or misrepresentation of another person's work.
- Knowingly introducing malicious code, security vulnerabilities, or harmful content.
- Spamming, advertising unrelated products, or off-topic discussions.
- Any unlawful or unethical conduct.

---

## Collaboration Guidelines

To maintain a productive engineering culture:

- Keep pull requests focused and easy to review.
- Write meaningful commit messages.
- Review code respectfully.
- Discuss technical disagreements professionally.
- Base decisions on evidence, engineering principles, and project goals.
- Prioritize maintainability, performance, and security.
- Document significant architectural decisions.

---

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it to the iTechie 360 maintainers or organization administrators.

When reporting an issue, include:

- A description of what happened.
- Relevant dates and times.
- Links or screenshots (where applicable).
- Any additional context that may assist in resolving the issue.

Reports will be handled confidentially whenever possible.

---

## Enforcement

Organization maintainers are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct fairly and consistently.

Depending on the severity of a violation, actions may include:

1. A private warning.
2. Temporary restriction from discussions or repositories.
3. Removal of repository permissions.
4. Suspension from the organization.
5. Permanent removal from the organization.

Serious violations may result in immediate removal without prior warning.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- All repositories within the iTechie 360 GitHub organization.
- GitHub Discussions.
- Pull Requests.
- Issues.
- Project Boards.
- Wikis.
- Organization-managed communication channels.
- Any official community events related to iTechie 360.

---

## Security

If you discover a security vulnerability, **do not disclose it publicly**.

Instead, privately contact the organization maintainers so the issue can be investigated and resolved responsibly.

---

## License and Contributions

By contributing to iTechie 360 repositories, contributors confirm that:

- They have the legal right to submit their contributions.
- Their work does not knowingly infringe on the intellectual property of others.
- They agree to the licensing terms of the repository they contribute to.

---

## Acknowledgements

This Code of Conduct is inspired by industry best practices, including the Contributor Covenant, and has been adapted to reflect the professional standards and values of iTechie 360.

---

**Together, we build technology with integrity, professionalism, innovation, and respect.**
